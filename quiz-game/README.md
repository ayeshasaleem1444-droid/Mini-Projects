# Quiz Game

A "how well do you know me?" friendship quiz built with HTML, CSS and JavaScript. Five personal questions — answer them and find out how well you really know me.

## How to play

1. Open `index.html` in a browser and press **Let's Start**.
2. Each question has 4 options — click one to answer. Your first click locks the answer, so choose carefully.
3. The quiz moves straight to the next question, and your score updates as you go.

## Scoring

- 1 point per correct answer, no negative marking — maximum score is 5.
- At the end you get your score out of 5, with a comment based on your percentage — from "You really know me." at 100% down to less friendly ones.
- Press **Do it bro!** on the result screen to play again.

## Notes

- No timer — take as long as you like.
- The 5 questions are fixed and always appear in the same order.
